# Recipe and photograph permissions

The [MIT license](LICENSE) covers this project's original software, instructions,
templates and synthetic demonstration content. It does not grant rights to
third-party recipe prose, book scans, photographs, illustrations, trademarks,
fonts or user uploads. Those materials retain their respective rights.

Only contribute material you created or have permission to redistribute under
its stated terms. Record author, source, permission/license and modifications
when adding permitted third-party material. A source citation does not itself
grant redistribution permission. Do not include a chef's private notes or
photographs without permission, even when names have been removed.

Public examples and test fixtures are fictional and visibly unreleased. They
show tool behavior, not a validated production process or food-safety approval.
Keep real recipe records, images and library identifiers outside public source,
archives, issue attachments and test logs.
