SMOKED EGGPLANT WITH TAHINI DRIZZLE AND FLAKE SALT

A smoked vegetable component finished with a cold-emulsified tahini drizzle. The eggplant is smoked whole, peeled and trimmed before weighing, so the grams on the card are the trimmed edible mass that reaches the plate, not the as-purchased mass. The drizzle seizes and loosens as it is stirred; endpoint is texture, not time.

Method

SMOKE

  1. 1

    Light the smoker with hickory chips and bring it to approximately 105 °C. Lay the eggplant lengths (E1) on the wire rack in a single layer, leaving space between each piece so smoke reaches every side. Brush the rack with grapeseed oil (E2) first so nothing sticks. Smoke for 22 minutes from the first chip, turning the eggplant once at 11 minutes, until the skin is blistered all over and the flesh gives way under a spoon tip. Consequential equipment: smoker, hickory chips, wire rack. Endpoint: blistered skin, tender flesh, no raw resistance.

  2. 2

    Transfer the smoked eggplant (E1) to a bowl, cover it tightly, and rest for 8 minutes so steam loosens the skin. Peel the skin off in strips and discard it. Cut the flesh into 6 cm lengths, weigh the trimmed flesh on the 0.1 g scale, and record the actual mass; do not backfill a guessed number if the trim differs from the reference.

Resist the urge to rinse the eggplant after smoking; rinsing washes the smoke off the flesh.

EMULSIFY

  1. 3

    Put the tahini (T1) in a small bowl. Add the lemon juice (T2) and the cold water (T3) in two additions, stirring between each addition. The mixture will seize and thicken before it loosens; keep stirring until it reaches a pouring consistency that coats a spoon and holds a trail for 3 seconds. The tahini basis is fixed at 90 g, so adjust only with lemon juice or water, never with more tahini, and record any adjustment in the trial notes.

  2. 4

    Taste the finished drizzle for balance. It should taste of tahini and lemon, with the salt coming from the flake salt at finish, not from the drizzle. Log any failed endpoint for trial review.

Cold water loosens the drizzle faster than warm water and keeps it from breaking.

FINISH & PORTION

  1. 5

    Arrange the smoked eggplant lengths on a warm plate in a single layer. Drizzle the tahini mixture evenly over the cut surfaces, about 8.0 g per portion. Sprinkle flake salt (F1) and cracked black pepper (F2) across the top immediately before service, so nothing sits in the salt long enough to draw moisture out of the eggplant.

  2. 6

    Weigh each finished portion at 8.0 g and log the usable mass against the 598 g reference yield. Any portion outside 7.5 g to 8.5 g is re-weighed, not approximated. Record the actual usable mass and transfer loss on every trial.

Portion weight is the control; plating arrangement is not.
