# Synthetic correction fixture

An original fictional demonstration. It continues the
[synthetic full-page intake](synthetic-full-page-intake.md) fixture with the
one thing that most often goes wrong in a long standardization: the cook
changes a number after the method has already been proposed.

## The correction

The cook replies to the proposal with a single correction and nothing else:

> Correction: the crumb is 120 g panko and 40 g sesame, but it is 12 g salt,
> not 8 g. Everything else you have is right. Also I weighed a tin of the
> crumb yesterday: 118 g.

## What this fixture is meant to prove

1. The newest statement wins. The salt row reads 12 g, and the old 8 g is
   gone from the card.
2. The correction is visible as a correction, not silently swapped.
3. The measured crumb yield — 118 g — is a measurement, and it is recorded as
   one, separate from the input arithmetic.
4. The still-unmeasured sauce yield stays unknown. One measured component does
   not license a number for the other.
